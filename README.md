# Hollow Oath 虚ろの誓い

**Seven lords rule the night. One oath to end them.**

A 3D anime-style boss-rush action game that runs straight in your browser, on phone, tablet or PC.

## ▶️ [PLAY THE GAME](https://idolarp.github.io/1game-/)

No download, no install. Tap the link and play.

---

### The story: *The Last Lamp*
The Order of the Lantern swore one rule: while a single lantern burns, the night does not win.
In one night, every lantern went out. Except yours.
Carry Wick, the last flame of the Order, through 12 chapters, 7 demon lords of the Hollow Court, and the Pale Sovereign himself.

### Features
- 5 playable hunters, each with their own skill, special and ultimate
- 12 stages: demon waves, 7 Hollow Court bosses and a final boss
- A full voiced story with cutscenes, a journal and ending credits
- Custom 3D engine with toon shading, bloom, soft shadows and god rays
- Works on phone (touch), PC (keyboard and mouse) and with controllers
- Picks the graphics quality to suit your device automatically

### Controls
| Action | Keyboard | Controller | Phone |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move | WASD | Left stick | Left thumb |
| Attack | J | X | ATTACK |
| Dodge | K | B | DODGE |
| Jump | Space | A | JUMP |

| Ultimate | Q | RT | ULT |
